An **Advanced Delegated Multi Signature Wallet** works like the
[Delegated Multi Signature Wallet](/groups/delegated-multisignature/): **you, the owner, can always pay alone**, and
**K of the N members** you choose can also pay without you. The difference is how the members sign: they sign
**together, online at the same time**, with MuSig2. Their payment then looks on the blockchain like a payment of
**one single key with one signature**, whatever the size of the group: the cheapest and most private payment of a
group.

:::note[A signing room is virtual]
The members sign in a **signing room**, which is **not a place**. It is a page of the app, the **Signatures** tab of
the group, that every member opens **in their own wallet, on their own computer, wherever they are in the world**.
The members never meet and never share a screen: each one only needs the app open and an internet connection
**during the same few minutes**. The app connects them through encrypted messages.

Think of it as a short online meeting: the group agrees on a time (by chat, phone or email), everybody opens the
group at that time, and the payment is signed in a couple of minutes.
:::

| | |
|---|---|
| Who can pay alone | **The owner** (the creator of the group), with one signature |
| Who else can pay | Any **K of the N members**, online at the same time (the owner is not one of the N) |
| Addresses | Taproot (they start with `bc1p`; with `tb1p` or `bcrt1p` on the test networks) |
| A payment of K members shows | One aggregated key and one 64-byte signature (instead of K keys and K signatures) |
| Minimum | At least 2 signers (with 1, use the [Delegated Multi Signature Wallet](/groups/delegated-multisignature/)) |
| Size of the group | Up to 500 combinations of K signers (for example 2 of 32, 3 of 15, 4 of 12, 8 of 12) |
| Standards | [BIP341](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0341.mediawiki) Taproot, [BIP327](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0327.mediawiki) MuSig2, [BIP386](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0386.mediawiki) / [BIP390](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0390.mediawiki) descriptors |

## Which one?

| | Signed one after the other ([Delegated](/groups/delegated-multisignature/), [Multi Signature](/groups/multi-signature/), [Legacy](/groups/legacy-multisignature/)) | Signed online at the same time (the **Advanced** wallets) |
|---|---|---|
| When do the signers sign? | Whenever each one can, hours or days apart | Online at the same time (each from wherever they are), within 3 minutes |
| Size of a payment of K signers | Grows with K | The same for any K |
| What the payment reveals | The K keys that signed | One key |
| Fee | Chosen by the first signer, with options for the last one | The network's at the moment of signing |

Choose an Advanced wallet when the signers can be **online at the same time** to pay - each from their own computer,
in any city or country: the savings of a family, the treasury of a team spread over several countries that agrees on
a time to pay. Choose the others when the signers are rarely online at the same time.

When nobody should pay alone, not even the creator, use the
[Advanced Multi Signature Wallet](/groups/advanced-multi-signature/).

## How it works

Each address has one small script for every combination of K members, and **each script is a single MuSig2 key**:
the [BIP327](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0327.mediawiki) aggregate of the keys of those K
people. The owner's own key is the Taproot *key path*: the owner pays with it alone, like a personal wallet. To pay
without the owner, K members run MuSig2 together:

1. **Round 1 - nonces.** Each signer's wallet creates a fresh secret number (a *nonce*) and sends its public part to
   the others. No password is needed for this, and it happens by itself.
2. **Round 2 - signatures.** Each signer types the password; the wallet makes a *partial signature* and sends it.
3. **Aggregation.** When every partial signature is there, the wallet of the last signer combines them into one
   Schnorr signature, checks it and sends the payment to the network.

The secret nonces **never leave the memory of the app** - not to a file, not to the browser - and each one is used
once. A secret nonce used twice could give away the private key, so if anything goes wrong (somebody leaves, the
time runs out, the app restarts) the session is closed and a new one starts with new nonces.

## Before you start

- You are [logged in](/online/).
- Every person you want in the group is an **accepted** [contact](/online/contacts/).

## 1. Create the group and invite the members (owner)

<Steps>

1. Open **Online → Smart Groups** and click **Create Group**. Choose the type
   **Advanced Delegated Multi Signature Wallet**, type a name and click **Create**.

   ![The group type Advanced Delegated Multi Signature Wallet and the name of the group](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/01-create-group.png)

2. Click **Add a contact** for each member. Type the **minimum** of members who must sign a payment without you
   (at least 2; you are not one of them) and click **Send invitation to group members**.

   ![Three members, a minimum of 2 and the button Send invitation to group members](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/02-members-and-minimum.png)

</Steps>

## 2. Accept the invitation (each member)

Each member opens **Online → Smart Groups** in their own wallet and clicks **Accept Invitation**.

![Accept Invitation in the list of Smart Groups](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/03-accept-invitation.png)

## 3. Activate the group (owner)

When every member has accepted, open the group and click **Activate Group**, then type your password. From now on
the members and the minimum do not change.

![All the invitations accepted and the button Activate Group](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/04-activate.png)

## 4. Receive

The **Wallet Balance** tab shows the next receiving address and its QR, the balance and the **Descriptors**. The tabs
**Addresses**, **Payment Requests** and **Transactions** work like the ones of your own wallet. **Rescan Balance**
looks up every address of the group again.

![The Wallet Balance tab of the group: its receiving address, the balance and Rescan Balance](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/05-wallet-balance.png)

## 5. The owner pays alone

Every payment of the group is made in its **Signatures** tab. The owner pays alone, with one signature and without a
signing room:

<Steps>

1. In **Signatures** click **Pay alone**.

   ![The Signatures tab of the owner, with the button Pay alone](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/06-pay-alone-button.png)

2. Type the amount, the destination and a description. Click **Estimate fee** and choose one of the three fees (or
   type one). Type your password and click **Pay**.

   ![The Pay alone form: amount, destination, description, the fee, the password and Pay](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/07-pay-alone-form.png)

3. The payment is signed and sent to the network at once.

   ![Transaction broadcast](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/08-paid-alone.png)

</Steps>

## 6. The members pay together: the signing room

Each member does these steps **in their own wallet**, from wherever they are; the app keeps everybody's screen up to
date by itself. Agree on a time first, so that the signers are online together.

<Steps>

1. **Open a payment session** (any member). In **Signatures** click **New payment**, type the amount, the
   destination and a description, and click **Open payment session**. Every member of the group receives it (a
   notification if they are on another page) and the session waits **15 minutes** for the signers to join. The one
   who opens a session needs the group's balance in his wallet: click **Rescan Balance** in **Wallet Balance** first.

   ![New payment: the amount, the destination, the description and Open payment session](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/09-new-payment.png)

2. **Join it** (the other signers). Open the group's **Signatures** tab in your own wallet. The tab lists every
   payment session of the group; the open one is selected, and below the list it shows what it pays, who has joined
   (*Joined (online now)*) and who has not yet (*Not joined yet*). Check the payment and click **I'm here** to say
   you are online and you agree with it. The session has its own **chat**, to ask or confirm anything while it is
   open.

   A member who is not one of your contacts shows as the beginning of their user name (for example
   `bcrt1pmwfqjj4x...`), never with the name somebody else gave them: see
   [What the members see of each other](/groups/#what-the-members-see-of-each-other).

   ![The open session in the Signatures tab of another member, with the button I'm here](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/10-join.png)

3. **Start signing** (the one who opened it). When enough members have joined, click **Start signing**. It does not
   sign anything yet: it fixes who signs (the members present), the coins and the fee - the network's fee at that
   moment. Every signer's wallet checks the payment, reads its coins from its own server and exchanges its nonces by
   itself.

   ![Every signer has joined, a line in the chat of the session, and the button Start signing](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/11-start-signing.png)

4. **Sign with password** (each signer). Click **Sign with password**, type your password and click **Sign**. The
   signers have **3 minutes**.

   ![The session ready to sign, with the button Sign with password](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/12-sign-with-password.png)

   ![The password and the button Sign](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/13-password.png)

   Until the last signature each signer sees *Signature sent*.

   ![Signature sent: the session waits for the other signers](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/14-signature-sent.png)

5. **Transaction broadcast.** The last signature completes the payment and sends it to the network: that signer
   sees *Transaction broadcast* with its transaction id, and the session shows *Paid* for everybody.

   ![Transaction broadcast, seen by the last signer](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/15-broadcast.png)

   ![The session paid, in the Signatures tab of the one who opened it](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/16-paid.png)

</Steps>

Anybody in the session can click **Leave**: it closes for everybody and its nonces are wiped. A session whose time
runs out closes by itself.

## 7. See the group in another wallet (descriptors)

In **Wallet Balance** click **Descriptors**. The group is described by two standard descriptors, one for the
receiving addresses and one for the change:

```
tr(OWNER/*,{pk(musig(xpubA/*,xpubB/*)),pk(musig(xpubA/*,xpubC/*)),...})
```

`musig()` is the BIP390 way to write a MuSig2 key. Bitcoin Core 30 and later derive every address of the group from
these descriptors; our tests check it on every release.

![The two descriptors of the group, written with musig()](../../../assets/screens/advdelegated/17-descriptors.png)

## Good to know

- **Online, not in person.** The signers never have to be in the same place: each one signs from their own wallet,
  anywhere in the world. What they share is the time: if a signer is not online during those minutes, the session
  cannot complete - open it again at a time that suits everybody, or use the
  [Delegated Multi Signature Wallet](/groups/delegated-multisignature/), which is signed one after the other.
- **Nothing secret travels.** A session only carries the payment, the public nonces and the partial signatures,
  encrypted for each member.
- **Back up your own wallet** as usual: your keys of the group come from it.
