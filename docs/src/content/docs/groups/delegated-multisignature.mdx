:::caution[Upgrading from an earlier version]
The next release builds the addresses of a Delegated group in a standard way that other wallets can reproduce.
They are **different addresses**. A group created with an earlier version will not show its old coins after the
upgrade: **send them out of the group before you upgrade**, then create the group again.
:::

With Delegated Multisignature you **delegate** the spending of a wallet to a group of people you trust, without
giving up your own control:

- **You, the owner, can always pay alone**, with one signature.
- **The members can pay without you** when the minimum number of them sign: K of the N members.

It fits money that must stay available if you are not: a family reserve, the funds of a company whose partners
can act when the director is away, an inheritance that some of your heirs can reach together.

If nobody should be able to pay alone, the owner included, use
[Legacy Multisignature](/groups/legacy-multisignature/): there every member is an equal signer.

| | |
|---|---|
| Addresses | Taproot (they start with `bc1p`; with `tb1p` or `bcrt1p` on the test networks) |
| Who can pay | The owner alone, or K of the N members together |
| Members | Any number, as long as the group has at most 2000 combinations of K members (for example 2 of 60, 3 of 23, 5 of 12) |
| Keys | From each person's own wallet; no private key ever leaves it |
| Standards | [BIP341](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0341.mediawiki) Taproot, [BIP342](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0342.mediawiki) scripts, [BIP386](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0386.mediawiki) / [BIP387](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0387.mediawiki) descriptors, [PSBT (BIP174)](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0174.mediawiki) |

In this guide **Alice** owns the wallet *Family savings*. **Bob**, **Carol** and **Dave** are the members, and
any **two of the three** can pay without Alice.

## Why it costs less and shows less

When the owner pays, the payment looks like any other Taproot payment: one signature, and nothing on the
blockchain says that a group exists.

When the members pay:

- **Only the keys of the members who signed are shown.** The members who did not sign stay hidden, and so does
  the size of the group.
- **The payment is smaller** than a classic multisignature one in most cases, so the fee is lower.

For measured sizes of many K-of-N combinations, see
[Bitcoin multisignature: a practical guide](/guides/multisig/).

## Before you start

- You are [logged in](/online/).
- Every person you want in the group is an **accepted** [contact](/online/contacts/).

## 1. Create the group and invite the members (owner)

<Steps>

1. Open **Online → Smart Groups** and click **Create Group**. Choose the type **Delegation MultiSignature**,
   type a name and click **Create**.

   ![The group type Delegation MultiSignature and the name of the group](../../../assets/screens/delegated/01-create-group.png)

2. Open the group. Click **Add a contact** for each member. Set the
   **minimum amount of users to approve a spend**: this is the K of "K of N". You are **not** one of the N: you
   pay alone.

   The minimum can be anything from 1 (any single member can pay) to all the members.

   Then click **Send invitation to group members**.

   ![The members, the minimum number of signers and the button Send invitation to group members](../../../assets/screens/delegated/02-members-and-minimum.png)

</Steps>

## 2. Accept the invitation (each member)

Open **Online → Smart Groups** in your own wallet and click **Accept Invitation** on the group's row.

![The invitation to the group in a member's list](../../../assets/screens/delegated/03-accept-invitation.png)

On accepting, your wallet sends the owner the public keys the group needs. No private key ever leaves a member's
wallet.

## 3. Activate the group (owner)

When every member has accepted, open the group and click **Activate Group**.

![All members accepted, and the button Activate Group](../../../assets/screens/delegated/04-activate.png)

The group now has two more tabs: **Wallet Balance** and **Signatures**.

## 4. Receive

Open the group's **Wallet Balance** tab. It shows the **receiving address** of the group, as text and as a QR
code. Give it to whoever pays the group.

![The Wallet Balance tab of the group with its receiving address](../../../assets/screens/delegated/05-wallet-balance.png)

After a payment, click **Update Balance**. The page shows the balance and one line per payment, and the group
moves to a new receiving address.

![The balance of the group after receiving, and the Send button](../../../assets/screens/delegated/06-funded.png)

The owner and every member see the same wallet in their own *Wallet Balance* tab.

## 5. A member starts a payment (the first signer)

Any member can start a payment.

<Steps>

1. In the **Wallet Balance** tab click **Send**.

2. Type the **amount**, the **address** to pay and a **description**. The description is what the other members
   will see, so say what the payment is for. Click **Next**.

   ![The payment: amount, address and description](../../../assets/screens/delegated/07-send-form.png)

3. Type your **wallet password** and click **Confirm**.

   ![The window that asks for the password](../../../assets/screens/delegated/08-password.png)

4. Choose the **fee**: one of the estimated levels in *Select Fee*, or tick *Manually select Fee* and type your
   own. This is the **calculated fee**.

5. Optional: tick up to **three extra fee options**. Each one is a percentage **above** the calculated fee:
   *+10%*, *+20%* and so on up to *+100%*.

   The members may sign hours or days apart, and by the time the last one signs the network may ask for a
   higher fee. With these options the last signer can pay more without starting again. You and every following
   signer sign all the options; only one is ever sent.

   Click **Send Coins**.

   ![The fee, the extra fee options +10% and +50%, and the Send Coins button](../../../assets/screens/delegated/09-fee-options.png)

6. The app shows **Signature added: your signature was added and forwarded to the other signers**. Nothing has
   been sent to the bitcoin network yet: the payment waits for the other signatures.

</Steps>

The payment is now in the **Signatures** tab:

![The Signatures tab with the payment waiting for more signatures](../../../assets/screens/delegated/11-signatures-waiting.png)

If the minimum of the group is **1**, there is nothing to wait for: you see no extra options, and your own
signature sends the payment.

## 6. Sign the payment (the following signers)

<Steps>

1. Open your wallet, open the group and go to the **Signatures** tab. The payment is there with its description,
   who signed it, the amount and the address. Click **Sign transaction**.

   ![The payment waiting in the Signatures tab of another member, with the link Sign transaction](../../../assets/screens/delegated/12-sign-transaction.png)

2. **Check the payment**: amount, address, description and fee. They cannot be changed, and they are read from
   the transaction itself, not from the message that brought it. If it is what you agreed to, click **Next**.

   ![The payment to check before signing, with the fee options](../../../assets/screens/delegated/13-check-payment.png)

3. Type your **wallet password**.

4. What happens next depends on how many signatures are still missing:

   - **More signatures are needed after yours.** The window lists the fee options the first signer set. You
     choose nothing: click **Send Coins**, and the payment goes on to the next signer.
   - **Yours is the last signature.** Choose the **Fee to pay**: the calculated fee, or one of the higher
     options if the network is busy. Click **Send Coins**. You sign only that transaction.

   ![The last signer chooses the fee to pay](../../../assets/screens/delegated/14-choose-fee.png)

</Steps>

When the last signature is added, the app shows **Transaction broadcast**: the payment has been sent to the
bitcoin network with the fee the last signer chose.

![The message Transaction broadcast](../../../assets/screens/delegated/15-broadcast.png)

The *Signatures* tab shows the payment as *Fully signed*:

![The payment fully signed in the Signatures tab](../../../assets/screens/delegated/16-signatures-finished.png)

## 7. The owner pays alone

The owner uses the same **Send** button of the *Wallet Balance* tab: amount, address, description, password and
fee. There are no fee options and nobody else signs. Click **Send Coins** and the payment is sent at once.

![The owner chooses the fee and sends](../../../assets/screens/delegated/17-owner-fee.png)

![The message Transaction broadcast for the owner](../../../assets/screens/delegated/18-owner-broadcast.png)

The owner does not sign the payments the members start: in the owner's *Signatures* tab they are listed, without
the link *Sign transaction*.

After **Update Balance**, everybody's *Wallet Balance* tab shows the new balance:

![The balance of the group after the two payments](../../../assets/screens/delegated/19-balance-after.png)

## 8. See the group in another wallet (descriptors)

A **descriptor** is a standard text that describes all the addresses of a wallet. With the two descriptors of the
group, any wallet that understands descriptors (Bitcoin Core, for example) shows the same addresses and the same
coins, **without our software**. It is your proof that the money does not depend on us.

<Steps>

1. In the **Wallet Balance** tab click **Descriptors**.

   ![The Descriptors button of the Wallet Balance tab](../../../assets/screens/delegated/20-descriptors-button.png)

2. Copy the two texts: one for the **receiving addresses**, one for the **change addresses**.

   ![The two descriptors of the group](../../../assets/screens/delegated/21-descriptors.png)

3. Import them in the other wallet. In Bitcoin Core, in a wallet created without private keys:

   ```
   importdescriptors '[{"desc":"<receiving descriptor>","timestamp":"now","range":[0,100],"active":true,"internal":false},{"desc":"<change descriptor>","timestamp":"now","range":[0,100],"active":true,"internal":true}]'
   ```

</Steps>

The descriptors hold only **public keys**: whoever has them can watch the group, never spend from it. They do
show every address of the group, so share them only with people who may see its balance. For a group with many
members the texts are long.

## Good to know

- **Signers do not have to be connected at the same time.** A payment to sign arrives when the member opens
  their wallet. If it is not in the *Signatures* tab yet, go back to the wallets page and open the wallet again
  a little later.
- **Check before you sign.** Sign only what you agreed to. The window shows the payment as it is in the
  transaction you are about to sign.
- **The owner is the strongest key.** Whoever has the owner's wallet can spend everything alone. Protect it like
  any wallet that holds the money, and consider a [Consensus Backup](/groups/consensus-backup/) for it.
- **The members are the way in if the owner is gone.** Choose N and K so that enough of them can still sign:
  2 of 3 survives the loss of one member, 3 of 5 the loss of two.
- **Backups.** Each person's keys for the group come from their own wallet. Someone who restores their wallet
  from their words can sign again.
