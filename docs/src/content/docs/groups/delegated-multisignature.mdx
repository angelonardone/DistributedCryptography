With Delegated Multisignature **several signatures are needed to approve a payment**. You stay the owner of the
group, and you delegate the approval of payments to a quorum of people you trust: family, business partners,
colleagues. No single person can move the money alone.

It is a good fit for joint accounts, company funds and any money that should need an agreement to be spent.

## Why it costs less than classic multisignature

In a classic multisignature wallet, every payment has to carry **all** the public keys of the group and all the
signatures. The more members, the bigger the payment and the higher the fee.

Our Delegated Multisignature uses our own implementation on **Taproot**. When the group pays:

- **Only the keys of the members who actually signed are shown.** The transaction is smaller, so the fee is
  lower.
- **The members who did not sign stay hidden.** With classic multisignature anyone looking at the blockchain can
  see every member's key and how the group is organised. Here they cannot.

For measured sizes of many K-of-N combinations, see
[Bitcoin multisignature: a practical guide](/guides/multisig/).

## How a payment gets approved

1. A member starts the payment from the group's **Wallet Balance** tab and signs it with their password.
2. The other members see it waiting in the group's **Signatures** tab and sign it too.
3. When the minimum number of members has signed, the payment is sent to the network.

Members do not have to be connected at the same time. Each signature travels to the others as an encrypted
message.
