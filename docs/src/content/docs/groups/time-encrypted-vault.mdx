**What happens when the person with all the passwords cannot log in?**

Business owners, freelancers and families manage countless digital accounts: bank portals, crypto wallets,
payroll, supplier contracts, personal files. If something happens to you, who can take over? Most people have no
plan, and the result is lost money, stopped operations or legal chaos.

A **Time-Encrypted Vault** is that plan. It is a backup of your wallet that a group you trust can open, but only
**after a date you set**, and only if **enough of them agree**. While you are around, you keep moving the date
forward and the vault stays closed. If one day you stop, the date arrives and the group can recover what you
protected.

It is estate planning, disaster recovery and access control in one system, and you keep custody the whole time:
no lawyers, no delays, and no third party holding your keys.

## How it works

A vault has two groups of people, and a date.

| | Who they are | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Restore group** | The people who will recover your wallet | They hold shares of the secret. A **minimum** number of votes is needed. |
| **Bounty group** | The people who "ring the bell" | After the date, any one of them can collect a small amount of bitcoin you left as a reward. Collecting it is what opens the time lock. |
| **Restore date** | A date from tomorrow onwards | Before it, nobody can open the vault. You push it forward for as long as you want the vault to stay closed. |

Opening the vault needs **both** things:

1. **The date has passed and the bounty has been collected.** The reward sits on the bitcoin blockchain, locked
   until the restore date. When a bounty member collects it, the transaction publishes the piece that was
   missing. The bitcoin network itself enforces the date, not our servers.
2. **The restore group reaches its minimum.** Enough members reveal their shares, as in a
   [Consensus Backup](/groups/consensus-backup/).

Neither group can open the vault with only its own part.

In this guide **Alice** creates the vault *Family vault*. **Bob** and **Carol** are the restore group (one vote
each, minimum two) and **Dave** is the bounty group.

## Before you start

- You are [logged in](/online/).
- Everyone you want in the two groups is an **accepted** [contact](/online/contacts/).

## 1. Create the vault (owner)

<Steps>

1. Open **Online → Smart Groups** and click **Create Group**. Choose the type **Time Encrypted Vault**, type a
   name and click **Create**.

   ![The group type Time Encrypted Vault and the name of the group](../../../assets/screens/tev/01-create-group.png)

2. Open the group. It has the **restore date** at the top and two tabs inside: **Restore Group** and
   **Bounty Group**.

   ![The new vault with the restore date and the two groups](../../../assets/screens/tev/02-vault-opened.png)

</Steps>

## 2. The restore group (owner)

In the tab **Restore Group**:

<Steps>

1. Click **Add a contact** for each person who will restore your wallet.
2. Set the **number of votes** of each one, and the **minimum amount of votes to recover the backup**.
3. Click **Send invitation to restore group members**.

</Steps>

## 3. The bounty group (owner)

In the tab **Bounty Group**, click **Add a contact** for each person who may collect the bounty, then click
**Send invitation to bounty group members**.

![The Bounty Group tab with one member and the button Send invitation to bounty group members](../../../assets/screens/tev/04-bounty-group.png)

The tab reminds you how it works: only after one of the bounty members collects the bounty can the restore
group restore the backup, and the bounty can be collected from the restore date on, with no time limit.

## 4. The restore date (owner)

Type the date in **Select the date when the backup became available to be restored**, or pick it with the
calendar, and click **Save**. The date must be tomorrow or later.

![The restore date, the restore group with its votes and minimum, and the Save button](../../../assets/screens/tev/05-restore-date.png)

## 5. Accept the invitation (every member of both groups)

Open **Online → Smart Groups** in your own wallet and click **Accept Invitation** on the vault's row.

![The invitation to the vault in a member's list](../../../assets/screens/tev/06-accept-invitation.png)

## 6. Activate the vault (owner)

<Steps>

1. When **everybody** in both groups has accepted, open the vault and click **Activate Groups**.

2. Type your **wallet password** and click **Confirm**.

   ![The window that asks for the password to activate the vault](../../../assets/screens/tev/08-activation-password.png)

3. The vault is **active**. It now has the button **Change Restore Date** and a second tab, **Bounty Wallet**.
   The app reminds you that **the bounty needs to be funded**.

   ![The active vault, with the button Change Restore Date](../../../assets/screens/tev/09-active.png)

</Steps>

On activation your wallet secret is split on your computer, and every piece is encrypted for the member who
keeps it.

## 7. Fund the bounty (owner)

<Steps>

1. Open the tab **Bounty Wallet**. It shows the date from which the bounty can be collected and the **receiving
   address** of the bounty.

   ![The Bounty Wallet tab with the address of the bounty](../../../assets/screens/tev/10-bounty-address.png)

2. Send a small amount of bitcoin to that address, from your wallet or from anywhere else. This is the reward
   for the bounty member who opens the time lock. Click **Update Balance** to see it.

   ![The bounty funded](../../../assets/screens/tev/11-bounty-funded.png)

</Steps>

The vault is now ready. **As the owner, you can take the bounty back at any time** with **Send**.

## Keep it closed: renew the date

Before the restore date arrives, open the vault and click **Change Restore Date**. Type your password and the
new date. The vault gets a new bounty address for the new date: move the bounty to it from the *Bounty Wallet*
tab.

This is your regular "I am still here". Pick a rhythm you can keep (every few months, for example) and put a
reminder in your calendar.

:::caution[If you forget, the vault can be opened]
That is exactly what it is for. If you let the date pass, the bounty group can collect the reward and the
restore group can then recover your wallet. If that happens by mistake, move your funds to a new wallet.
:::

## When the date has passed

### A bounty member collects the bounty

<Steps>

1. Open the vault. Your only tab is **Bounty Wallet**. From the restore date on, click **Send**.

   ![The Bounty Wallet tab of a bounty member, with the Send button](../../../assets/screens/tev/12-bounty-member.png)

2. Type the **address** where you want to receive the bounty (an address of your own wallet, for example) and
   click **Next**. The whole bounty is sent.

   ![The address to receive the bounty](../../../assets/screens/tev/13-collect-form.png)

3. Type your **wallet password**. Then choose the **fee** and click **Send Coins**.

   ![The fee and the Send Coins button](../../../assets/screens/tev/14-collect-send.png)

</Steps>

The app shows **Coins submitted successfully**. Once this payment is on the blockchain, the time lock is open.

Before the restore date the bitcoin network rejects this payment, so there is nothing to collect early.

### The restore group recovers the wallet

<Steps>

1. Each member who takes part opens the vault in their own wallet. After the bounty has been collected, the
   button **Restore Wallet** is there. Click it.

   ![The vault as a member of the restore group sees it, with the button Restore Wallet](../../../assets/screens/tev/16-restore-wallet.png)

2. The app shows **The group has been notified**.

   ![The message The group has been notified](../../../assets/screens/tev/17-first-signed.png)

3. When the **minimum** has been reached, the app shows **The group has been RESTORED** and the button
   **Create recovered wallet** appears. Click it.

   ![The button Create recovered wallet](../../../assets/screens/tev/18-create-recovered-wallet.png)

4. Type a **name** for the recovered wallet and a **new password**, twice. Click **Restore Wallet**.

   ![The name and the new password of the recovered wallet](../../../assets/screens/tev/19-recovered-wallet-form.png)

5. The recovered wallet is in the list of wallets of that member's computer.

   ![The recovered wallet in the list of wallets](../../../assets/screens/tev/20-recovered-in-list.png)

</Steps>

It is the owner's wallet: the same addresses, the same bitcoin, the same user name. Open it and click
**Update Balance** to load its payments.

![The recovered wallet opened, with the owner's first address](../../../assets/screens/tev/21-recovered-balance.png)

If **Restore Wallet** is not there yet, the bounty has not been collected, or its payment is not on the
blockchain yet. Go back to the wallets page and open the wallet again a little later.

## What you are trusting

- The **date** is enforced by the bitcoin network: the bounty cannot be collected early.
- The time lock binds the bounty group and the restore group **separately**. If people from *both* groups agreed
  to work together against you, the date would not stop them. Put different people in each group, and people
  you trust.
- As with a Consensus Backup, once the minimum is reached **any member of the restore group** can create the
  recovered wallet.
