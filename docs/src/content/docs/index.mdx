## Everyone approves from their own app. Nothing to pass around.

<p class="dc-lead">
	Sharing a backup, co-signing a payment or leaving an inheritance usually means exporting files, carrying them
	from one wallet to the next and hoping everybody knows the procedure. Here, each person simply opens their
	app.
</p>

<Compare oldLabel="The usual way" newLabel="With Distributed Cryptography">
<Fragment slot="old">
<ol>
	<li>One person prepares the transaction and exports it as a file.</li>
	<li>The file goes to the first signer by e-mail, USB stick or QR code.</li>
	<li>That signer imports it into their own wallet, signs, and exports it again.</li>
	<li>It goes on to the next signer. And the next.</li>
	<li>Someone collects the signatures, combines them and sends the payment.</li>
</ol>
<p class="note">Everyone needs the same tools and the same know-how, and nobody can see where it got stuck.</p>
</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="new">
<ol>
	<li><strong>You start the payment</strong> in the app.</li>
	<li><strong>Each signer opens their own app</strong>, finds the request waiting, checks it and approves with their password.</li>
	<li><strong>The last approval sends it</strong> to the network.</li>
</ol>
<p class="note">No files to move, no meeting to arrange. Requests travel end-to-end encrypted, and everyone sees who has already signed.</p>
</Fragment>
</Compare>

It is the same for **recovering a vault** and for **an inheritance**: the people you chose approve from their own
computers, in another city or another country, whenever they open the app. The app does the rest.

## What self-custody alone cannot do

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Recover your vault with people you trust" icon="heart">
		**Consensus Backup** splits the master key of your vault among friends, family or company officers. Nobody can
		restore it alone. Enough of them together can, each from their own app, and you are told as soon as someone
		starts.

		On any computer, your bitcoin comes back with everything you keep online: your contacts and all your groups,
		with their shared wallets, backups and passwords.

		[How Consensus Backup works](/groups/consensus-backup/)
	</Card>
	<Card title="Plan for the day you are not there" icon="clock">
		A **Time-Encrypted Vault** stays closed while you keep renewing it. If you stop, the group you chose can open
		it after the date you set. No lawyers, no notary and no third party holding your keys.

		[How the Time-Encrypted Vault works](/groups/time-encrypted-vault/)
	</Card>
	<Card title="Let others pay when you cannot" icon="puzzle">
		With **Delegated Multisignature** you keep paying alone, and K of the N people you trust can pay without you.
		Built on Taproot: lower fees, and the blockchain shows only the people who signed.

		[How Delegated Multisignature works](/groups/delegated-multisignature/)
	</Card>
	<Card title="Require agreement for every payment" icon="approve-check">
		With **Legacy Multisignature** nobody pays alone: K of the N members approve each payment. The app collects
		the signatures for you; there is nothing to export or combine.

		[How Legacy Multisignature works](/groups/legacy-multisignature/)
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## How it works

<Steps>

1. **Install it on your own computer.** Windows, Mac, Linux or Docker. Create your vault: the keys are made
   there and stay there, encrypted with your password.

2. **Connect with the people you trust.** You log in anonymously, with a key of your wallet instead of your name
   or e-mail, and add them as contacts.

3. **Create a Smart Group.** Choose who is in it and how many of them must agree. From then on, every backup,
   payment and recovery is an approval in their app.

</Steps>
