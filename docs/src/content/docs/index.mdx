## What you can do with it

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Keep your own keys" icon="padlock">
		The wallet runs on your PC or server (Windows, Mac, Linux, Docker). Keys are created and used there,
		encrypted with your password. There is no account to open and nothing to trust us with.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Recover your wallet with people you trust" icon="heart">
		**Consensus Backup** splits your wallet secret among friends, family or company officers. A minimum number of
		them must agree before it can be restored, and you are told as soon as someone starts.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Plan for the day you are not there" icon="clock">
		A **Time-Encrypted Vault** stays closed while you keep renewing it. If you stop, a group you chose can open
		it after the date you set. No lawyers and no third party holding your keys.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Share control of the money" icon="puzzle">
		**Delegated Multisignature** needs K of N people to approve a payment. It is built on Taproot, so it costs
		less in fees than classic multisig and shows less about the signers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Encrypt more than bitcoin" icon="document">
		Encrypted notes, files, passwords and authenticator backups, a secret splitter and QR codes. They all work
		offline, on your computer.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Talk and share in private" icon="comment">
		End-to-end encrypted chat and files between users. You log in anonymously: your user name is a key of your
		wallet, not your e-mail.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Public source code, standard Bitcoin

Everything is built on published standards (BIP 32, 39, 44, 49, 84, 86, 341 and 342) and on well-known
algorithms (Argon2id, AES-256-GCM). The code is public, so anyone can audit it or build it themselves.
