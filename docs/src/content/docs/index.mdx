<p class="dc-kicker">The safety net</p>

## The key stays with you, and you are never the only one who can get it back

<p class="dc-lead">
	Two mechanisms, invented here, turn the people you trust into a safety net. Each of them acts from their own
	app, wherever they are. Nobody has to understand how it works: they open the app and approve.
</p>

<div class="dc-mechanism">

### Consensus Backup <span class="dc-badge">Patent pending</span>

Your vault can be recovered by people you choose, but **only if enough of them agree**. The secret of your vault
is cut into pieces and each piece is encrypted for one person. You decide how many pieces are needed: five people
and any three of them, for example. Two alone learn nothing. You are warned the moment someone starts a restore,
and you can stop it.

<BackupDiagram />

On any computer, the recovered vault brings back your bitcoin, your identity, your contacts and all your groups,
with their shared wallets, backups and passwords. [How Consensus Backup works](/groups/consensus-backup/)

</div>

<div class="dc-mechanism">

### Time-Encrypted Vault <span class="dc-badge">Patent pending</span>

**What happens when the person with all the passwords cannot log in?** Business owners, freelancers and families
manage countless accounts, wallets and documents. A Time-Encrypted Vault is the plan: it stays closed while you keep
renewing a date, and if you stop, the people you chose can open it after that date. Not before. The date is enforced
by the Bitcoin network itself, so nobody can open it early: not the group, and not us.

<VaultTimeline />

It is estate planning, disaster recovery and access control in one, and you keep custody the whole time: no lawyers,
no notary, and no third party holding your keys. [How the Time-Encrypted Vault works](/groups/time-encrypted-vault/)

</div>

## How it works

<Steps>

1. **Install it on your own computer.** Windows, Mac, Linux or Docker. Create your vault, with the keys in a file on
   that computer or on Ruby. The offline tools work from this moment, with no connection.

2. **Connect with the people you trust.** You log in without giving a name or an e-mail: the app identifies you
   with a key of your own vault. Add them as contacts.

3. **Create a Smart Group.** Choose who is in it and how many of them must agree. From then on, every backup,
   payment and recovery is an approval in their app.

</Steps>
